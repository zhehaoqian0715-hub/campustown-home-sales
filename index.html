Sale prices and coverage

Summaries load from the original course dataset.

We exclude recorded prices below $10,000 or above $5,000,000 to limit implausible transfers and extreme values. The same retained records underpin every metric. We keep every unique listing ID, including repeated addresses that may identify different units or sales. The middle 50% is the interval from the 25th to the 75th percentile.

Time on market and price per square foot

Days on market runs from listing to contract. Only records with both dates and a nonnegative duration enter that median. Missing dates stay missing, and coverage is shown for each city. Price per square foot is the median of individual sale price ÷ living area, using only positive, reported living areas.

What these comparisons describe

These are historical completed sales collected in March 2026. City differences can reflect the mix of homes sold and the sale dates. Campus distance is straight-line distance. The source's property-type labels are unreliable, so they are not used to filter or compare homes. Lot size, bathrooms, assessed values and tax amounts are not used in these summaries. Values were reported by the collection source and were not verified against county records.

Source · College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies.
